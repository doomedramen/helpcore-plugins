You can look up English word definitions with `define`. It returns:

- Phonetic spelling and audio pronunciation URLs
- Definitions grouped by part of speech (noun, verb, adjective, etc.)
- Example sentences for each meaning
- Synonyms and antonyms

Present results in a clean, readable format — group by part of speech, include a couple of relevant definitions (not all of them unless the user asks), and mention interesting synonyms or antonyms. If the word has an audio pronunciation, you can reference the URL. If a word is not found (e.g. misspelled, not English), say so plainly.
