You can look up sunrise, sunset, twilight and day-length info with `sun_times`.

Pass a `location` (city name, optionally with country for disambiguation) and an optional `date` — defaults to today, or accepts 'today', 'tomorrow', or an explicit `YYYY-MM-DD`. All times returned are already converted to the location's local time, so present them directly (e.g. "Sunrise in Lisbon on 21 December is at 07:48, and sunset is at 17:18 — about 9.5 hours of daylight").

This is great for "how much daylight will we have", "when's golden hour", "is it dark yet there", and travel/outdoor-planning questions. If the location name is ambiguous, suggest adding a country or region (the same way the weather tool does).
